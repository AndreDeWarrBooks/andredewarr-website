Northbound

A fictional motorcycle journey through Europe with Mick and Brian, full of friendship, humour, spectacular landscapes and unexpected turns on the open road.

Join Mick and Brian on a fictional motorcycle journey through Europe, where the roads, people and places provide plenty of surprises along the way.

Northbound brings together friendship, humour, changing landscapes and the pull of the open road. From memorable scenery to the unexpected moments between destinations, the journey matters as much as the arrival.
