About the Author

André De Warr

They say history is written by the victors, the famous, and the extraordinary. But what about the rest of us?

I was born in Worcester in 1976. I live in Scotland now, but Worcester will always be part of my story. I only began writing in 2026, after a difficult period in my life, and discovered how much I had to say.

My books don't all fit neatly onto one shelf. The Troubles and Tribulations in the Life of a Nobody comes from my own life; The Long Road Home mixes real-life experience with fiction; and Northbound and What We Leave Behind are entirely fictional. Northbound is especially full of friendship, travel and humour.

I'm still learning with every book.

When someone reads one of my books, I want them to picture the characters, understand what they're going through and feel as though they're right there beside them. I want readers to lose themselves in the story, almost as if they're living it themselves.

Delivery, returns & privacy

Information for customers buying signed books and merchandise directly from André De Warr Books.

Delivery

Direct orders for signed books and merchandise are currently available for delivery to UK addresses only.

  • Signed-book, mug and bookmark prices exclude postage; UK postage is added separately.
  • Orders placed with a non-UK delivery address will be cancelled and refunded.
  • International customers can order standard copies through the Amazon links on this website.
  • Orders are usually dispatched within 3–5 working days. Pre-order items are dispatched once the relevant print batch is ready.
  • Delivery details are collected securely during PayPal checkout.

Returns & cancellations

You may normally cancel an online order within 14 days of receiving it, without giving a reason.

  • Signed or personalised books prepared specifically for your order are not eligible for a change-of-mind return or refund.
  • This does not affect your statutory rights if a signed or personalised book is faulty, damaged, incorrect or not as described.
  • For other eligible items, please email us within 14 days of delivery if you wish to cancel.
  • After telling us you wish to cancel, please return the item within 14 days.
  • Unless the item is faulty, damaged or incorrect, you are responsible for return postage.
  • Refunds are processed in line with your statutory consumer rights.

Privacy

We use the personal information supplied with your order only where needed to process and fulfil your purchase, answer enquiries and meet legal or accounting obligations.

  • This may include your name, email address, delivery address and order details.
  • Payment processing is handled by PayPal; we do not receive your full card or bank details.
  • Order information may be shared with postal or delivery providers only where needed to send your purchase.
  • Information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for fulfilment and legal or accounting purposes.
