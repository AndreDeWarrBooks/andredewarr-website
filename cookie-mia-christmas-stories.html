Cookie & Mia: Christmas Stories

Available now · Paperback · Ages 4–9

Two little sisters. Ten big adventures. One magical Christmas!

Meet Cookie, a cheeky cream Shihpoo with a nose for mischief, and Mia, her loving black-furred sister. From snowy surprises and magical Christmas markets to Santa’s visit and a very squeaky pudding, the two sisters discover the joy of Christmas together.

Ten heartwarming, funny festive stories, perfect for bedtime, cosy afternoons and reading together as a family.
